Shared customers with one balance (D16), optional central warehouse (D17), cross-branch quantities (D18), transfer workflow (D19) and accounting modes (D22), central purchasing, consolidated reports, owner view of everything (D15).

## Phase 10 - Apps: Customer App (white-label), Website, staff mobile app
One white-label base (D9, D29) and the website: catalogue, ordering, prescription photo, order tracking, notifications. Build pipeline that produces a pharmacy's app from its name and identity, ready to publish on the **pharmacy's own** App Store / Google Play accounts. Offered from the commercial launch. **Staff mobile app (D34, 10.5):** the staff system as native Android and iOS apps (Capacitor, same React code) published under our company account, plus a PWA; built only once the desktop is fully mature; a paid add-on enforced by the licence (device count) and the Cloud.

## Phase 11 - Compliance depth
Track and trace at dispensing, controlled-drug register, recall by batch (scope from Phase 0 research; moves earlier if regulation requires).

## Phase 12 - Commercial launch
Logo and visual identity (the name is fixed, D30), macOS and Linux releases (D27), code signing on Windows and macOS (D28), customer-app onboarding per pharmacy (D29), onboarding, subscriptions/licences, import service, support tooling, handbooks, external penetration test, pricing.

## Ordering principles
Retire the biggest risks first (offline/sync, the Hub on three platforms, printing, tenant isolation, compliance) in Phase 0. Build the Chain-ready data model from Phase 2 although the Chain edition ships in Phase 9. Anything that touches money or stock is a ledger with audit.
