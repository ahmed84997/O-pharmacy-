# eStock Pharmacy - Screen and Feature Inventory (v1)

10 Nov 2026. Source: 19 screenshots sent by the owner from the pharmacy's live install. Purpose: the baseline for the Phase 1 "eStock-like" demo and for the "his way" redesign. Customer names visible in the screenshots are deliberately not copied here.

## 1. Identity
- Product: **e-Stock Pharmacy Ver 1.8.4 Full**, by **Modern Soft Company** (support line printed on the home screen).
- Windows desktop application, Arabic RTL, classic menu bar plus a right-hand shortcut bar.
- Deployment at the pharmacy (owner): one "Master" PC holds the program/data; the other PC runs the installed app and connects to it.
- Status bar: current user ("صاحب المؤسسة"), login time. A scrolling ticker at the bottom shows item lines like "عدد 1.00 - <item> - تنتهي 01/2027" (looks like an expiry/stock alert ticker - **to confirm with the owner**).

## 2. Navigation
**Menu bar (12):** البيانات العامة - الأصناف - المخازن - الموردين - المشتريات - العملاء - المبيعات - الحسابات اليومية - الحسابات العامة - الطلبيات - شئون العاملين - إطار.
**Shortcut bar (6):** النواقص - الأصناف - المشتريات - المبيعات - توريد نقدي - العملاء.
**Size:** about **156 menu entries**, of which about **77 are reports or statements** (counted from the screenshots).

## 5. Domain model implied by eStock
Organisation and settings - Branch with internal stores (مخازن) - Items (units with conversion factors, dosage, form, manufacturer, group, location, barcodes, sale price, cost, reorder level, active/stopped, drug/non-drug, service/stock) - Batches by expiry (expiry shown per line) - Suppliers (prices, opening balances, statement) - Customers (types, classes, regions, credit limit, contracts, employee link) - **Contracts (التعاقدات)** = corporate/insurance-style accounts billed separately with paper contract invoices - Sales invoices (types: cash, card, credit, held, home delivery) and returns, item swap - Purchase invoices and returns (with or without invoice), bonus, tax - Stock transfers between internal stores, stocktake (ad-hoc and periodic), cost adjustment, opening balances - Cash drawers, safes, banks, card accounts, cheques (in/out, due dates) - Chart of accounts, journal, income statement, financial position, shareholders, capital, profit distribution - HR: jobs, employees, permissions, attendance, absence/leave, deductions, incentives, advances, commissions, payroll - Delivery agents - Login log.

## 6. Observations for the redesign (analysis, not facts)
1. **Report sprawl:** about half the menu is separate reports. A single report centre with filters, saved views and export would replace most of them.
2. **Destructive actions:** «حذف» buttons on items, customers, lines and shortages; eStock shows "deleted" items, so some soft-delete exists. Our rule: No-Delete everywhere (cancel / void / archive, audited).
3. **Cost and profit on the cashier screen:** should be permission-based (owner/manager only).
4. **Contracts are insurance-lite:** contract customers, contract invoices and reports exist; no claims workflow, approvals or reconciliation seen.
5. **No e-receipt / ETA, no track-and-trace** visible in the menus.
6. **Manual "Update System" and local backups** - our cloud gives automatic updates and off-site backups.
7. **Ordering is thin** (reorder level, prepare order, shortage notebook) - YK already goes much further (customer requests, PO stages, receipt, AI invoice audit, returns).
8. **HR/payroll overlaps YK** (attendance, deductions, advances, salaries) - YK's version is the base.
9. **Multi-store awareness exists inside one branch** («المخازن الداخلية للفرع», transfers between stores, «المتوفر في مخازن أخرى»); nothing shows cross-branch features.
10. **Keyboard-first speed** is the habit pharmacists have with eStock - the new cashier screen must be at least as fast by keyboard and barcode.
