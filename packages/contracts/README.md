# @o-pharmacy/contracts (Phase 6, Sprint 6.4)

Contract customers (insurers and companies) and their claims. Migration `ctr_0001_contracts_claims`. It does not touch the POS: a claim refers to a sale only by a reference (`saleRef`), so cover can be applied at the till later without changing this package. The screens behind this (the contracts and insurance screens of the old system) were never seen, so the rules follow ARD module 7 and the market research; the owner should check them against his real contracts.

- **`ContractService`** (`contract.manage` to change, `contract.view` to read): a contract has a payer type (insurance or corporate), a validity period and **terms versioned by date** (coverage percent, optional cap per claim, an amount above which a claim needs approval, days the payer takes to pay). New terms never rewrite old claims. Members (insured cards) are switched off, never deleted; items can be excluded from cover; suspend or end a contract with a reason (ended is final).
- **`ClaimService`**: `quote` and `create` split a basket by the terms on the service date (excluded items are not covered, the cap applies; rounding to the piaster) and save the split (what the payer owes and what the member pays) with the claim, which never changes afterwards. A claim above the approval amount waits for someone else with `contract.manage`. State machine: pending approval, approved, batched, paid / partial / rejected, cancelled (reason).
- **Batches and reconciliation**: `submitBatch` (`insurance_claim.submit`) sends approved claims of one contract to the payer and sets the expected payment date; `reconcile` (`insurance_claim.reconcile`) records, claim by claim, what was accepted (a reason when less than claimed); the money received must equal the accepted total. Paid in full = paid, less = partial (the rest is written off), none = rejected, and a rejected claim can go into a later batch. `outstanding` shows what each contract owes: awaiting approval, ready to send, sent by age (0-30, 31-60, 61+ days) and overdue batches.
- **Money**: whole piasters. Posting the received money into the ledger is a wiring step (a `claim.batch_reconciled` event carries it).
- No new permission: `insurance_claim.submit` is off by default for pharmacists (grant it per person).
