# @o-pharmacy/ops - Operations (Phase 5, built on the proven YK domain, D5)

Sprint 5.1: customer requests and shortages. Plugs into the Hub after inventory and POS (`[...MIGRATIONS, ...POS_MIGRATIONS, ...OPS_MIGRATIONS]`; migration `ops_0001_requests`).

- **Two kinds, one lifecycle:** a *customer request* (CR-, a customer is waiting and must be told; `customer_request.create`) and a *shortage* (NQ-, out of stock, nobody waiting; `shortage.record`). Every item starts at `pending_purchase`. Items are catalogue items or free text; quantity at least 1; the same idempotency key is the same request.
- **The item machine** (`states.ts`, on the shared `StateMachine` in core): 13 states (the original 9 plus `stocked`, `out_for_delivery`, `unavailable`, `closed_unavailable`), no `ordered -> pending_purchase` edge. The header status (open, partially fulfilled, completed, closed, mixed closed) is derived from the items and never stored.
- **`move`** is the one place an item changes state: shared-machine check, a reason for every undo, reopen or cancel, then item + event + audit + outbox in one transaction. The later sprints put their flows on it (orders and receiving 5.2, delivery 5.3) and decide who may do which edge.
- **Now:** `cancelItem` / `cancelRequest` (reason, `customer_request.cancel`), the supplier-cannot-provide flow (`markUnavailable`, `markAvailable`, `closeUnavailable` after the customer is told, `reopenUnavailable` managers only), `claim` / `release` / `assign` (one person at a time, managers assign), `comment`, `addItem`, `updateRequest` (the owner changes only by a manager), `get`, `list` (kind, derived status, owner, customer, search), `myWork`, `timeline` (state changes with who and why, comments).
- **No-Delete:** requests, items, events and comments are never deleted; what was asked for (item, quantity) never changes: cancel and add another. Events and comments are append-only.
- No new permission: it uses `customer_request.*` and `shortage.*`; a manager is a role check (O, A, M), as in YK.

## Orders and notifications (Sprint 5.2, migration `ops_0002_orders_notifications`)
- **`OrderStageService`**: a sent purchase order goes through ordered, received, counted, entered, labelled, shelved (ported from YK). Each stage is stamped once with who, when and minutes since the previous one, and must follow the one before. `link` attaches a request item to an order (pending purchase to ordered), `receive` moves linked items to awaiting verification, `count` needs a result for every item (arrived goes to ready to notify, or stocked for a shortage; missing or damaged goes back to pending purchase with a note). `markEntered` needs a posted supplier invoice on the order: native stock replaces YK's manual eStock step.
- **`NotificationService`**: `toNotify` lists customer items waiting to be told with a fixed Arabic message; `notify` records who told the customer and by which channel (phone, whatsapp, sms, in person) and moves the item to notified. The system never sends to a real number by itself.
- Append-only: stages, count results, notifications, item-order links. No new permission: `purchase_order.*`, `receiving.verify`, `notification.view/send`.

## Delivery, pickup and tasks (Sprint 5.3, migration `ops_0003_handover_tasks`)
- **`DeliveryService`** (on `RequestService.move`, `customer_request.update`): `dispatch` (address required, notified to out for delivery), `deliver` (receiver name required; pickup from notified, home from out for delivery), `failDelivery` (back to notified, reason), `markNotCollected` (reason), `reopen` (managers only, reason). Each step is an append-only `item_handovers` row. `board` lists items out for delivery and notified items waiting longer than N days (default 7).
- **`TaskService`** (`staff_tasks.manage` / `staff_tasks.my`): create (idempotent key, T- numbers), reassign, complete (assignee or manager), cancel (reason), `mine`, `list` with an overdue flag. A task is never deleted, its title never changes and a closed task never changes.
- No new permission.

## Attendance (Sprint 5.4, migration `ops_0004_attendance`)
- **`AttendanceService(hub, rules?)`**: `setSchedule` (weekly pattern from a date, append-only, overnight shifts end the next day), `checkIn` / `checkOut` (`attendance.record_own`; late, early, worked and overtime minutes are worked out and saved; a check-out more than `maxOpenHours` after the check-in is refused as a wrong day), `decideOvertime`, `correct` (reason required, old times kept in `attendance_events`), `requestLeave` / `decideLeave` / `pendingLeave` (approved full-day leave makes the day a leave day), `markAbsences`, `mine`, `team` (month totals; overtime counts only when approved), `history`. Managers use `attendance.approve` and never decide or correct their own day.
- Rules (time zone, grace minutes, minimum overtime, max open hours) are injected (`DEFAULT_ATTENDANCE_RULES`), not hardcoded in the logic.
- Pay, penalties and salary cycles are NOT here: finance is Admin only and comes with the reports phase. Cash closing stays in the POS package (`ClosingService`).
